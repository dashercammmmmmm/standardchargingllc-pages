Rough Mark Classic Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Rough Mark Classic (RMC) is an Android scoring app for rodeo bull and bronc judges, published by Standard Charging LLC. This policy explains what the app handles and why.

Information the app handles

  • Scoring data you enter: rodeo names and dates, rider and stock details, judge names, and scores. This is stored on your device.
  • Judge sync (optional): if you start or join a multi-judge session, the event details, judge display names, seat assignments and scores for that session are sent through Google Firebase so the judges' devices stay in sync. Sync uses an anonymous Firebase sign-in that creates a random technical identifier. You do not create an account or give an email address.
  • Text recognition: if you import an image or PDF to fill in rodeo details, its text is read on your device using Google ML Kit. The file is not uploaded by the app.

RMC does not use advertising, advertising identifiers, analytics, in-app purchases or location data, and does not sell personal information.

How information is used

Only to record and review scores, run multi-judge sync when you choose it, keep the service secure, and fix problems.

Sharing

Session data is visible to the judges in that session, under access rules that keep each judge's scores hidden from the others until they have scored. Firebase (Google) processes sync data on our behalf. We do not share data with anyone else except when required by law.

Retention and deletion

Data on your device stays until you delete it. Sync join codes expire within 48 hours. You can remove your data at any time with Delete my data in the app’s Settings, which deletes your local data and your sync session data and anonymous identifier from Firebase. To ask us to delete data, email the address below.

Security

Sync data is encrypted in transit and protected by access rules that limit each session to its own judges.

Children

RMC is intended for adult rodeo judges and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
